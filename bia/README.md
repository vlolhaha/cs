# Bi-a 8 Bi (8-Ball Pool)

Game bi-a 8 bi mot file HTML duy nhat, chay truc tiep tren trinh duyet (desktop + mobile).

## Tai nhanh

    wget https://github.com/vlolhaha/cs/releases/latest/download/bi-a.html

Sau do mo bi-a.html bang trinh duyet.

## Tinh nang

- Nguoi vs Nguoi / Nguoi vs May
- 4 do kho: De, Vua, Kho, Vo dich
- Physics bi that: ma sat lan, xoay (spin), va cham dem, va cham bi-bi
- Diem danh bi trang (spin widget), thanh can goc sieu min, thanh luc
- Luat 8 bi: chia nhom tron/soc, loi, bi cai roi lo, dat bi tu do
- Ho tro cam ung (xoay ngang dien thoai)

## Bot AI

- Ghost-ball: tinh diem cham thay vi nham thang tam bi
- Gioi han goc cat, kiem tra duong bi chan (2 chang)
- Danh gia rui ro bi cai roi lo sau va cham (scratchRisk)
- Chi danh bi 8 khi da don het nhom
- Co cu danh an toan (safety) khi khong co ban sach
- Mode Vo dich: khong nhieu, dat bi tu do bang cach quet toan ban chon vi tri tot nhat
